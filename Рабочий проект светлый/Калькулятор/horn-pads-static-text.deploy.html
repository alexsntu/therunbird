About the Corvette C6 Airbag Cover

Every RunBird airbag cover is handmade to order for the C6 Corvette (2005–2013), fitting C6 Standard, Z06, and ZR1 trims. Choose synthetic Nappa leather, genuine Alcantara®, or a leather + Alcantara combo, then pick a Shield, Circle, or Tee stitch design with your choice of stitching color. An optional custom center emblem (gloss or matte, several logo choices) can be added for +$75.

Pricing starts at $355 and every build is made to order in 7–14 business days. Before your cover ships, we send real photos of the finished piece for your approval.
