About the Corvette C6 Drift Shift Knob

The RunBird Drift is a handmade shift knob for the C6 Corvette's manual 6-speed shifter, with a taller, elongated shape built as separate upper and lower sections. Choose synthetic Nappa leather or genuine Alcantara® for the lower section, and synthetic Nappa leather, Alcantara®, or carbon fiber for the upper section, with your choice of stitching color. The top style is available in carbon fiber (black, blue, red, or titanium), Alutex, or a custom finish, in gloss or matte.

Pricing starts at $220 and increases based on the options you choose — for example, a carbon-fiber upper section adds $40, and a colored carbon-fiber top adds $20–$30. Every build is made to order in 7–14 business days, and before your shift knob ships, we send real photos of the finished piece for your approval.
