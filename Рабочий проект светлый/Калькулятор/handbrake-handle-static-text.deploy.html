About the Corvette C6 E-Brake Handle

The RunBird E-Brake Handle is a handmade replacement e-brake handle for the C6 Corvette, with a matching carbon-fiber release button. Choose synthetic Nappa leather, perforated synthetic Nappa leather, or genuine Alcantara® for the top and bottom sections, with your choice of stitching color. The bottom section can also be built in carbon fiber (black, red, blue, titanium, or Alutex, in matte or gloss).

Pricing starts at $195 and increases based on the options you choose — for example, a carbon-fiber bottom section adds $55, and adding a carbon-fiber release button adds $50. Every build is made to order in 7–14 business days, and before your handle ships, we send real photos of the finished piece for your approval.
