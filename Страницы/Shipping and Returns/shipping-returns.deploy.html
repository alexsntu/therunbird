Shipping Details

Where We Ship

Anywhere in North America — the United States and Canada. Enter your address at checkout to see it confirmed.

Shipping Cost

Calculated at checkout based on your location and order size, before you pay — no surprises later.

Nothing Ships Early

Your order only ships after production is finished and you've approved the photos we send you.

Tracking Number

Sent to your email the moment your package leaves our workshop, so you can follow it the whole way.

Status Updates

We keep you informed at every stage — from photo approval to shipped to delivered — not just a single tracking link.

Careful Packaging

Every piece is packaged to protect its finish in transit, and arrives with an installation guide and material care card.

✕ No Returns On Custom Orders

Every piece is built by hand to your exact spec — material, finish, color, and thread — the moment you order it. Because of that, we can't accept returns for a completed order that matches what you configured.

  • No returns for change of mind once production has started
  • No returns for a correctly-built order matching your configuration
  • You approve photos before it ships, so there are no surprises on arrival
✓ We'll Make It Right

If something goes wrong on our end or in transit, we stand behind it — no cost to you. Contact us with your order number and photos, and we'll sort it out.

  • Arrived damaged in transit — we remake it, free
  • Doesn't match your order specification — we remake it, free
  • Manufacturing defect discovered after install — contact us and we'll make it right
