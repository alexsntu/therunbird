Install Steps

Four Steps. No Surprises.

01
Unbox & Inspect

Check your part against the included guide and confirm nothing was damaged in transit before you start.

02
Remove The Factory Part

Take off the original piece you're replacing. Keep the factory hardware — you'll reuse most or all of it.

03
Install Your RunBird Part

Bolt it to the same factory mounting point. No trimming, no drilling — it's built to sit exactly where the original did.

04
Reconnect & Test

Reattach any connectors, test that everything still functions as expected, and you're done.
