How It Fits

Built For A Direct Swap

We only make accessories that replace a factory part with one of our own — never an add-on that gets taped, zip-tied, or wedged into place. Every RunBird piece is measured against the original factory part it's replacing, so it lands on the exact same mounting point using the same hardware.

That means no cutting, no drilling, and no rewiring. Your factory functions — horn, controls, clips, connectors — carry over and keep working exactly as they did before. Nothing to improvise, nothing left over when you're done.

  • Mounts to your Corvette's original factory hardware and mounting points
  • No cutting, drilling, or permanent modification required
  • Fully reversible — remove it and your factory part goes right back on
  • Every order ships with a step-by-step guide for that exact part
