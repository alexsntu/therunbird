Shipping & Delivery

We ship across North America — anywhere in the United States and Canada. Enter your address at checkout and shipping options for your location will be shown automatically.

Shipping cost is calculated at checkout based on your location and the size of your order, before you pay.

Once your build leaves our workshop, transit time depends on your location — typically a few business days within North America. You'll receive a tracking number as soon as it ships.

You'll get a tracking number by email the moment your order ships. You can also look up any order using the Track Order tool in the site header or footer.
