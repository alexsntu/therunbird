Returns, Warranty & Support

Since every piece is custom-made to your exact spec, we can't accept returns on configured orders. If your item arrives damaged or doesn't match what you ordered, we'll remake it at no cost.

Contact us with your order number and photos of the damage as soon as you notice it. We'll sort out a remake or replacement — you won't be left with a damaged part.

We stand behind every piece we build. If something fails due to a manufacturing defect rather than wear and tear, contact us and we'll make it right.

Reach out through our Contact Us page with your order number and question — our team built these parts by hand, so we can usually help right away.
