Material 01

Carbon Fiber

Aerospace-grade carbon fiber, laid up in a distinctive 2×2 twill weave — the same pattern-and-weight class used in motorsport and aircraft interiors. It's stiff, lightweight, and shows a deep, dimensional pattern under light instead of a flat print.

Every carbon piece is finished with multiple coats of lacquer and wet-sanded by hand between coats, then buffed to either a deep gloss or a low-glare matte — your choice.

Weave 2×2 Twill
Finish Gloss or Matte
Colors Black, Red, Blue, Gold, Silver
Used On Steering wheels, shift knobs, handbrakes, side skirts
