Materials Guide

Three Materials.
Zero Compromise.

Every RunBird piece is built from exactly three materials — aerospace carbon fiber, premium synthetic Nappa leather, and genuine Alcantara®. No cheap filler materials, no shortcuts. Here's what each one is, where it comes from, and how to keep it looking new.

Carbon Fiber Nappa Leather Alcantara® Care Guide
Material 01

Carbon Fiber

Aerospace-grade carbon fiber, laid up in a distinctive 2×2 twill weave — the same pattern-and-weight class used in motorsport and aircraft interiors. It's stiff, lightweight, and shows a deep, dimensional pattern under light instead of a flat print.

Every carbon piece is finished with multiple coats of lacquer and wet-sanded by hand between coats, then buffed to either a deep gloss or a low-glare matte — your choice.

Weave 2×2 Twill
Finish Gloss or Matte
Colors Black, Red, Blue, Gold, Silver
Used On Steering wheels, shift knobs, handbrakes, side skirts
Material 02

Nappa Leather

Premium synthetic Nappa leather — engineered to match the look and feel of the quality tier used in high-end European sport car interiors. It's finished to stay soft rather than stiff, so it wraps cleanly around tight curves like a steering wheel rim without wrinkling or cracking over time.

Available smooth or perforated for extra grip and breathability, and hand-stitched with a contrast thread color of your choice.

Grade Premium Synthetic Nappa
Texture Smooth or Perforated
Stitching Hand-stitched, any thread color
Used On Steering wheels, shift knobs, horn pads, handbrakes
F1-Grade Feel
Material 03

Alcantara®

Genuine Alcantara® — the material of choice for Formula 1 steering wheels and exotic car interiors. It's a suede-like microfiber that grips your hands far better than leather, especially under hard cornering or in the heat.

It also cuts glare under direct sunlight, which matters more than it sounds like on a wheel rim or dash you're staring past all day. We use it on its own or mixed with Nappa for a two-tone grip zone.

Type Genuine Alcantara® Microfiber
Feel Suede-like, high-grip, low-glare
Combinations Solo or mixed with Nappa leather
Used On Steering wheels, shift knobs, horn pads
Keep It Looking New

Care Guide

Carbon Fiber
  • Wipe with a soft microfiber cloth and a little water or mild glass cleaner
  • Add a UV-protectant wax every few months to keep the gloss from dulling
  • Don't use abrasive pads or ammonia-based cleaners
  • Don't scrape ice or debris off directly — it can dull the lacquer
Nappa Leather
  • Wipe down with a damp microfiber cloth to remove dust and oils
  • Use a proper leather conditioner every 1–2 months to keep it supple
  • Keep it out of direct sun when parked to prevent fading and drying
  • Don't use household cleaners, alcohol, or baby wipes
Alcantara®
  • Brush lightly with a soft suede brush to lift the nap and remove dust
  • Blot spills immediately with a dry cloth — don't rub
  • For deeper cleaning, use a dedicated Alcantara® cleaner and let it air-dry
  • Don't soak it or use leather conditioner on it
