Nappa Leather

Full-grain Nappa leather — the same quality tier used in high-end European sport car interiors. Nappa is tanned to stay soft rather than stiff, so it wraps cleanly around tight curves like a steering wheel rim without wrinkling or cracking over time.

Available smooth or perforated for extra grip and breathability, and hand-stitched with a contrast thread color of your choice.

Grade Full-Grain Nappa
Texture Smooth or Perforated
Stitching Hand-stitched, any thread color
Used On Steering wheels, shift knobs, horn pads, handbrakes
