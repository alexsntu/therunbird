Alcantara®

Genuine Alcantara® — the material of choice for Formula 1 steering wheels and exotic car interiors. It's a suede-like microfiber that grips your hands far better than leather, especially under hard cornering or in the heat.

It also cuts glare under direct sunlight, which matters more than it sounds like on a wheel rim or dash you're staring past all day. We use it on its own or mixed with Nappa for a two-tone grip zone.

Type Genuine Alcantara® Microfiber
Feel Suede-like, high-grip, low-glare
Combinations Solo or mixed with Nappa leather
Used On Steering wheels, shift knobs, horn pads
