Keep It Looking New

Care Guide

Carbon Fiber
  • Wipe with a soft microfiber cloth and a little water or mild glass cleaner
  • Add a UV-protectant wax every few months to keep the gloss from dulling
  • Don't use abrasive pads or ammonia-based cleaners
  • Don't scrape ice or debris off directly — it can dull the lacquer
Nappa Leather
  • Wipe down with a damp microfiber cloth to remove dust and oils
  • Use a proper leather conditioner every 1–2 months to keep it supple
  • Keep it out of direct sun when parked to prevent fading and drying
  • Don't use household cleaners, alcohol, or baby wipes
Alcantara®
  • Brush lightly with a soft suede brush to lift the nap and remove dust
  • Blot spills immediately with a dry cloth — don't rub
  • For deeper cleaning, use a dedicated Alcantara® cleaner and let it air-dry
  • Don't soak it or use leather conditioner on it
