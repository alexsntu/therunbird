01

Information We Collect

When you place an order, create an account, or contact us, we collect the information needed to build, ship, and support that order:

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone number
  • Shipping & billing address — to deliver your order and process payment
  • Order details — the products, configurations, and materials you choose
  • Account information — if you create an account, your login and order history
  • Support & warranty communication — messages, photos, or details you send us for a claim or question

Payment card information you enter at checkout is sent directly to our payment provider to process the transaction — we don't receive or store your full card number.

02

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to run the business you're interacting with:

  • Process, build, and ship your order
  • Send you order confirmations, production updates, photo approval requests, and tracking information
  • Respond to support, installation, and warranty requests
  • Maintain your account, if you create one
  • Understand overall site traffic and usage, in aggregate, to improve the site

We don't use your information to build advertising profiles, and we don't sell it.

03

Cookies & Site Analytics

Our site uses cookies that are necessary for it to function — keeping items in your cart, remembering you're logged in, and completing checkout. We also use our store platform's built-in analytics to understand overall traffic (like which pages are visited and how many orders come through).

We don't use third-party advertising trackers or pixels (like Meta Pixel or Google Ads) — nothing on this site is set up to build ad-targeting profiles of you.

04

Who We Share It With

We keep this short because our answer is short: we share your name and shipping address with the carrier that delivers your order — and no one else.

  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone, for any reason
  • We do not share your data with marketing or advertising partners
  • Payment details you enter are handled directly by our payment provider to process your transaction — we don't pass your card data to any other party

The only exception is if we're legally required to disclose information — for example, in response to a valid court order.

05

How Long We Keep It

We keep your order and account information for as long as needed to fulfill your order, support any warranty claim, and meet our own legal and accounting obligations. If you'd like your data deleted sooner, see Your Privacy Rights below.

06

Your Privacy Rights

We ship within the United States and Canada, and this policy is written for customers there. California residents in particular have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and we apply the same principles to all our customers regardless of state:

  • Right to know — ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Right to delete — ask us to delete your personal information, subject to our order and legal record-keeping needs
  • Right to opt out of sale — not applicable here, since we don't sell personal information to begin with
  • Right to correct — ask us to fix inaccurate information on your account

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Contact Us below.

07

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. That said, no method of transmission or storage online is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

08

Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll remove it.

09

Changes To This Policy

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and change the effective date at the top. We encourage you to check back occasionally.

10

Contact Us

Questions about this policy, or want to exercise any of the rights above? Reach out through our Contact Us page and we'll get back to you.
