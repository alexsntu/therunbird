✓ What's Covered

For 12 months from the day your order is delivered, we'll repair, remake, or replace your piece if the problem comes down to how it was built.

  • Stitching failures or seam separation under normal use
  • Material defects in the carbon fiber, Nappa leather, or Alcantara® present from delivery
  • Carbon fiber cracking, delamination, or clear-coat failure not caused by an impact
  • Hardware or fitment issues traced back to how the piece was built
✕ What's Not Covered

This warranty covers workmanship, not wear. It doesn't cover damage caused by use, time, or how the piece was installed.

  • Normal wear and tear — fading, minor scuffing, leather softening with age
  • Damage from improper or unauthorized installation
  • Damage from accidents, misuse, or aftermarket modification
  • Damage from extreme weather, prolonged direct sun, or improper cleaning products — see our Materials Guide for care instructions
