If Something's Wrong

How To File A Claim

01
Contact Us

Send us your order number along with photos and a short description of the issue — the more detail, the faster we can help.

02
We Review It

Our team compares what you're seeing against how that piece was built and our workmanship standards to confirm it's covered.

03
We Make It Right

If it's a covered defect, we'll repair, remake, or replace your piece at no cost — whatever fixes it properly.
