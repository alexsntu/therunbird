Why We Started

A Workshop, Not A Warehouse

RunBird started in 2015 out of frustration — we couldn't find Corvette interior parts that actually felt right. Everything on the market was either generic, poorly fitted, or mass-produced overseas. So we set up our own workshop and started making the parts ourselves.

Every order that comes in is built from scratch, by hand, for that one customer. We don't keep a warehouse of finished pieces waiting to ship — we cut, wrap, and stitch after you order, in the exact material, color, and thread you chose.

That's slower than pulling something off a shelf. It's also the only way we know to make something that actually feels custom.

“No two RunBird pieces are ever exactly the same — because no two orders are.”
