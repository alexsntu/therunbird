About the Corvette C6 Shift Top Cap

The RunBird Shift Top Cap is a handmade replacement top cap that fits your existing C6 Corvette shift knob — no need to replace the whole knob. Choose a carbon-fiber finish (black, blue, red, or titanium), Alutex, or a custom finish, in matte or gloss. A shift-pattern logo and color inlay round out the build.

Pricing starts at $55 and increases based on the options you choose — for example, a colored carbon-fiber top adds $20. Every build is made to order in 7–14 business days, and before your top cap ships, we send real photos of the finished piece for your approval.
