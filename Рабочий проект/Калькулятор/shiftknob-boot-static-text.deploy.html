About the Corvette C6 Shift Knob Boot

The RunBird Boot is a handmade replacement shift knob boot cover for the C6 Corvette — no knob included, just the boot. Choose synthetic Nappa leather, genuine Alcantara®, a synthetic Nappa leather and Alcantara mix, or textured synthetic Nappa leather, with your choice of stitching color. Available for both manual 6-speed and automatic transmissions.

Pricing starts at $55. Every build is made to order in 7–14 business days, and before your boot ships, we send real photos of the finished piece for your approval.
