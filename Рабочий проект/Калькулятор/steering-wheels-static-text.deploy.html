About the Corvette C6 Custom Steering Wheel

Every RunBird steering wheel is handmade to order for the C6 Corvette (2006–2013). Choose a D-shape or F1-shape rim, then pick your material for the grips, top, and bottom sections: plain leather, perforated leather, genuine Alcantara®, or carbon fiber. Stitching color, center mark, and — on 2007–2013 models — carbon fiber spoke covers with audio/bluetooth cutouts are all configurable.

Pricing starts at $765 and increases based on the options you choose — for example, carbon fiber trim sections add $220 each, and CF spoke covers add $165. Every build takes 7–14 business days, and before your wheel ships, we send real photos of the finished piece for your approval.
