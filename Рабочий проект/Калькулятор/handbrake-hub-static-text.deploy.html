About the Corvette C6 E-Brake Line

Every RunBird e-brake accessory is handmade to order for the C6 Corvette (2005–2013). Choose from three styles — Handle + CF Button, Knob Boot, and Handle Button — in synthetic Nappa leather, genuine Alcantara®, or carbon fiber. The Knob Boot is available for both manual and automatic-transmission cars; the Handle Button is a standalone carbon-fiber or black finish button that fits your existing handle.

Pricing starts at $50 and every build is made to order in 7–14 business days. Before your e-brake accessory ships, we send real photos of the finished piece for your approval.
