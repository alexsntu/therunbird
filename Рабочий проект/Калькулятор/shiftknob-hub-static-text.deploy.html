About the Corvette C6 Shift Knob Line

Every RunBird shift knob is handmade to order for the C6 Corvette (2005–2013). Choose from five styles — Classic, Ball, Drift, Top Cap Only, and Boot — in synthetic Nappa leather, genuine Alcantara®, or carbon fiber. Classic, Ball, and Drift are built for manual 6-speed shifters; the Boot cover is available for both manual and automatic transmissions.

Pricing starts at $55 and every build is made to order in 7–14 business days. Before your shift knob ships, we send real photos of the finished piece for your approval.
