About the Corvette C6 Classic Shift Knob

The RunBird Classic is a handmade round shift knob for the C6 Corvette's manual 6-speed shifter, paired with a matching carbon-fiber top cap. Choose synthetic Nappa leather, perforated synthetic Nappa leather, or genuine Alcantara® for the knob body, and pick a carbon-fiber finish (black, blue, red, titanium, or Alutex) or a custom finish for the top cap, in matte or gloss. A shift-pattern logo and color inlay round out the build.

Pricing starts at $170 and increases based on the options you choose — for example, a carbon-fiber back section adds $35, and a colored carbon-fiber top cap adds $20. Every build is made to order in 7–14 business days, and before your shift knob ships, we send real photos of the finished piece for your approval.
