About the Corvette C6 Ball Shift Knob

The RunBird Ball is a handmade round ball-shaped shift knob for the C6 Corvette's manual 6-speed shifter, built for a shorter, grippier throw than the Classic knob. Choose synthetic Nappa leather, perforated synthetic Nappa leather, or genuine Alcantara® for the knob body, and pick a carbon-fiber finish (black, blue, red, titanium, or Alutex) or a custom finish for the top cap, in matte or gloss. A shift-pattern logo and color inlay round out the build.

Pricing starts at $250 and increases based on the options you choose — for example, a colored carbon-fiber top cap adds $20. Every build is made to order in 7–14 business days, and before your shift knob ships, we send real photos of the finished piece for your approval.
