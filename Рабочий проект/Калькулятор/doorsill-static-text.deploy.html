About the Corvette C6 Carbon Fiber Door Sills

Every RunBird door sill set is handmade to order for the C6 Corvette. Choose a carbon-fiber finish (black, blue, red, or titanium), Alutex, or a custom finish, in matte or gloss, and pick a logo design (or no logo) with your choice of color.
